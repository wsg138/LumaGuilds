# V3 icon direction

The primary navigation art must read like a custom Minecraft resource pack, not abstract software glyphs.

- **Members & Ranks:** two Minecraft player busts, a guild pennant, gold rank crown/chevrons.
- **Money & Vault:** reinforced chest, gold ingots, visible lock plate.
- **Level & Quests:** XP bottle, enchanted/quest book, progression sparkle.
- **Homes & Land:** small Minecraft house, grass block/land base, compass.
- **Allies & War:** shield, crossed swords, teal/red faction accents.
- **Parties & LFG:** three player busts, small party pennant, social/plus accent.
- **Customize Guild:** guild banner, dye/brush tool, pattern/sparkle accent.
- **Guild Settings:** redstone/mechanical assembly, comparator-like controls, lit indicators.

Shared rules: transparent 64x64 canvases, top-left light, deliberate dark outlines, 3-4 material shades where space allows, strong silhouette, no baked card or title, and enough negative space to remain readable at inventory scale.
